# ADR-085 Governed speaking assessment
Status: Accepted for MVP baseline

Context: Recorded speaking assessment must follow Step 1 governance while preserving primary-audio and derived-transcript evidence.
Decision: Reuse generic subjective entities and RubricVersion with declarative evidence modalities, exact media/transcription provenance, bounded evidence validation, mandatory HumanReviewRequest, and deterministic Decimal aggregation.
Consequences: AI cannot approve or release results.
Alternatives considered: Autonomous audio scoring, rejected.
