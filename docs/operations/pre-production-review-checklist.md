## P1 cross-partition submission integrity milestone

This is a separate release-blocking milestone. The current E05 correction addresses definite-rejection recovery inside the existing same-browser protocol.

The accountable roles are the runtime/backend engineer and the security/product reviewers. Their appointment and human acceptance remain open. Review either server-visible editing and submission coordination or an enforceable single-writer policy with an explicit takeover and recovery contract. A revision check against responses already persisted on the server cannot identify an answer that remains unsent on another device.

Acceptance requires independent browser storage partitions and authenticated sessions on disposable PostgreSQL. Hold or fail a replacement in one partition, then attempt submission from another. The policy must prevent silent finalization of the older response set or require an explicit, accurately explained resolution. Test offline operation, partition loss, expired leases, simultaneous submit/edit, takeover, restart and recovery. Retain immutable response revisions, tenant isolation and release confidentiality. Record exact-SHA CI and independent disposition before closing this milestone or authorizing the affected pilot use.
