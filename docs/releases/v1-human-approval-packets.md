# V1 human approval packets

These are unsigned decision requests. The accountable person records their identity and authority, independence or conflicts where relevant, exact scope and revision or environment, evidence links, actual date, decision, limitations and follow-up. Capture an authenticated permalink or approved record reference in the [register](v1-readiness-register.md). Role names below are proposed decision roles. No holder or signature is implied.

For retrospective review, the repository owner may appoint a qualified human who did not prepare or merge the change and can withhold acceptance. The reviewer records their own authenticated decision. Accept, require corrective action with the exception open, or withhold acceptance with the exception open. Successful CI supplies technical evidence for this decision.
