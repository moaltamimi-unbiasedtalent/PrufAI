# V1 readiness register

**Product signoff PENDING. Pilot launch authorization NO. Production readiness NO.**

This register has 28 gates: 6 engineering, 12 human decisions, 7 operational verifications and 3 assessment validity gates. H08, H11 and A03 are tracked beyond the proposed pilot. E01 records the passing main baseline and the outstanding check of the eventual pilot release candidate. An authorized owner must record the evidence revision and decision at closure. Proposed role owners are assignments to make, and the register names no appointed people.

| Evidence class | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| TECHNICAL_TESTED | A named test or rehearsal ran on an exact revision and configuration. |
| HUMAN_APPROVED | An authorized human recorded the dated decision and scope. |
| OPERATIONALLY_VERIFIED | Evidence came from the intended environment with accountable operators. |
| LEGALLY_AUTHORIZED | The authorized decision-maker recorded the applicable rights, privacy, processing or official-use determination. |
| ASSESSMENT_VALIDATED | Qualified assessment review established which claims the exact item form and scoring rules support. |

Use **NOT VERIFIED** when proof is unavailable. **OPEN** requires action. **DEFERRED** tracks an item outside the proposed V1 scope. Passing CI supplies technical evidence only.
